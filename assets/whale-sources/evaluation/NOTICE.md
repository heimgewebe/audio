# Independent evaluation source

## NOAA PMEL Alaska humpback whale call

- Source: NOAA Pacific Marine Environmental Laboratory, *Alaska humpback whale call*
- Recording: winter 1999
- Source page: https://www.pmel.noaa.gov/acoustics/whales/sounds/sounds_akhump.html
- Distribution page: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Akhumphi1x.ogg
- License: public domain, work originating from the U.S. National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration
- Raw SHA-256: `54a91b3c3e488941697acdf01face985ad149ca91e5d85af0f3ec8b1ad00ab42`

The distributed source is accelerated tenfold. The evaluation derivative restores nominal speed, selects a fixed two-second interval in accelerated source time, converts it to 20 seconds at nominal speed, filters it to the analysis band and adds short fades. This source is locked for final external evaluation only and is excluded from model construction, runtime trajectory selection and parameter tuning.
